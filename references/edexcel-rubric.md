# Edexcel EPQ Assessment Reference

Use this reference for Edexcel Level 3 Extended Project work when the unit is P301 Dissertation or P302 Investigation/Field Study. It is distilled from the supplied Pearson documents `Project-Specification-Level-3-P301 Dissertation.pdf` and `Project-Specification-Level-3-P302 Investigation.pdf`, both labelled Issue 3, September 2019. The supplied board documents are the authority for this local task; verify a newer document when the user provides one.

## Mark architecture

Each unit is marked out of 54:

| Assessment objective | Maximum | What the top band must show |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| AO1 Manage | 9 | A focused question/hypothesis refined independently; a clear, detailed rationale and ordered plan; detailed activity evidence; progress monitored and the plan adjusted when needed. |
| AO2 Use resources | 12 | A wide range of relevant sources; appropriate, consistent referencing and bibliography; careful selection; analysis and synthesis linked to the question; reliable-source judgement that distinguishes fact, speculation, and opinion. |
| AO3 Develop and realise | 24 | A coherent, consistently clear structure; a well-supported argument or interpretation; careful treatment of counterarguments/alternative explanations or data patterns/models; a developed conclusion, further work, and wider implications where relevant. |
| AO4 Review | 9 | High insight and self-awareness; detailed analysis of methodological and interpretive limits; justified changes for next time; perceptive conclusions about the research and writing process; clear presentation and confident, informed answers where presentation evidence is assessed. |

Use the board's bands, not invented weighting:

- AO1: 0-3, 4-6, 7-9.
- AO2: 0-4, 5-8, 9-12.
- AO3: 0-8, 9-16, 17-24.
- AO4: 0-3, 4-6, 7-9.

An exact mark should be provisional unless the full evidence set and assessor context are available. A polished paper cannot compensate for missing management evidence, weak source use, or a generic evaluation.

## Unit-specific reading

### P301 Dissertation

The product is a literature-based dissertation. The central chain is:

`focused research question -> planned research and source evaluation -> literature analysis/synthesis -> lines of reasoning and counterarguments -> conclusion -> evaluation`

High-band P301 work should place the question in a wider research context, connect sources to one another, and use the distinction between fact, speculation, and subjective opinion when judging reliability. Do not require an original survey or experiment unless the student independently chooses to add one and can support it ethically and methodologically.

### P302 Investigation/Field Study

The product must make the investigation visible:

`focused question or hypothesis -> method and contingency plan -> independent data collection -> analysis/calculation/model -> interpretation -> conclusion and evaluation`

High-band P302 work should show successful, independent data-collection techniques, carefully selected resources/data, detailed links to the question, perceptive analysis of trends or patterns using relevant models/theories, and appropriate mathematical calculations. Check whether the sample, instrument, procedure, data quality, and analysis justify the claims.

## Evidence mapping

- AO1 evidence normally sits across Proposal, Gantt/timeline, dated Activity Log, milestones, and visible plan changes.
- AO2 evidence normally sits across the research/resource record, in-text citations, bibliography, source-evaluation table, and the paper's synthesis.
- AO3 evidence normally sits in the paper or investigation outcome, figures/tables where relevant, conclusion, and suggestions for further work.
- AO4 evidence normally sits in Evaluation/Reflection and presentation record/evidence. It must evaluate the process and interpretation, not merely describe enjoyment or list skills.

If the evidence is not present in the supplied files, label the AO as `not reliably assessable` rather than treating a blank template field as evidence of failure.
